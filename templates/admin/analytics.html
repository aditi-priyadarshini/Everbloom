Artisan Performance Dashboard

Group customers by sign-up month, track repeat purchase rates, and calculate Lifetime Value (LTV) to focus retention efforts where they matter most.

Compare each component's average recorded wastage % against its historical baseline. Flag batches that exceeded the norm so root causes can be investigated.

Combine material cost, labour, wastage losses, and actual sale price per product to rank products by true profit margin — not just revenue.
06
Return Reason Analytics
Categorise returns by reason (damaged, wrong item, quality issue). Chart trends over time and link back to specific artisans or raw material batches.
